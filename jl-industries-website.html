The Breakdown Dilemma

When a textile ring frame or production gearbox shears a gear or scores a drive shaft, replacement spares from European or Japanese OEMs often take 8 to 14 weeks and cost exorbitant freight. Many older models are completely discontinued with zero drawings.

The JL Industries Solution

Bring or courier your worn sample to Sitra. We extract the exact module, pressure angle, and bearing clearances. We select proper EN19 / EN24 alloy or phosphor bronze, machine to ±0.015mm, induction harden the teeth, and deliver a ready-to-run drop-in replacement within 24 to 48 hours.

Quality Assurance & Metrology

Zero-Defect Dimensional Verification Lab

Precision is not an assumption—it is verified. Every shaft, bearing seat, gear bore, and thread leaving our workshop is measured with calibrated digital micrometers and granite reference plates.

Mitutoyo Digital Micrometers

Resolution: 0.001 mm (1 Micron)

External micrometers covering 0–25mm, 25–50mm, 50–75mm, and 75–100mm. Calibrated to measure precision h6 shaft seats and bearing tolerances.

Granite Surface Plate (Grade 0)

Flatness: < 3.0 µm

Heavy laboratory granite surface table paired with a 300mm Mitutoyo digital height gauge for concentricity, runout, and planar step verification.

Bore Dial & Air Plug Gauges

Internal Accuracy: ±0.002 mm

Three-point internal bore micrometers and dial bore indicators ensuring H7 bore tolerances on bronze bushings and cast bearing housings.

Surface Roughness & Pitch Tests

Ra Testing: 0.2 to 6.3 µm

Electronic surface roughness tester ensuring oil-seal journals meet Ra 0.8µm, alongside full sets of metric, BSW, and Acme thread pitch gauges.

Working Process

From Drawing to Finished Part

A simple, transparent 3-step workflow designed to save machine downtime.

01

Send Drawing or Sample

Send an engineering blueprint, rough CAD sketch, PDF drawing, or high-res photos with callouts on WhatsApp. If you don't have a drawing, drop off the worn part at our Sitra shop.

02

Quote & Prototype

We provide a transparent quotation with timeline. For complex fits, we machine a prototype first piece so you can test fit and check clearance in your assembly before proceeding.

03

Production & Dispatch

Once the prototype is verified, we run your required batch quantity with strict quality inspections on critical dimensions, deburr, and package for immediate pickup or delivery.
